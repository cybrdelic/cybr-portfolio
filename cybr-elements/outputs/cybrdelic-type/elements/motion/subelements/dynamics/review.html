Dynamics rebuild review

Implemented without Houdini, using Warp, NumPy/SciPy, Blender Cycles/Bullet, and existing liquid caches. These are simplified graphics solvers and authored effects, not reproductions of Houdini’s proprietary implementations or a claim of photoreal quality.

LavaCrust stays on the moving surface, opens gaps over hot material, and darkens as transported heat decreases. The early hot nozzle still looks too uniform, and the underlying flow is a retained viscous liquid cache with one-way crust coupling.
MetalA reflective sheet folds, buckles and retains deformation, then falls out of frame. Surface highlights establish metal much more clearly than the earlier particles; some folds remain unnaturally smooth.
FoamFine whitewater follows the liquid surface and separates into spray, replacing the earlier large-bead appearance. It reads as foamy water rather than a fully resolved wet foam. Inherited liquid-domain boundaries remain visible late in the clip.
IceAdvected microfracture detail adds a frosted surface and the frozen phase breaks apart. The carrier still produces liquid-like contours and thin torn sections; this remains below the desired photoreal standard.
CombustionA narrow reacting jet ends in a short outward burst, then loses luminosity as fuel is consumed and the gas cools and mixes. Reducing downward source momentum removed the smooth falling fireball. The burst still has a soft silhouette and needs more resolved small-scale structure; this low-Mach solve does not reproduce a compressible blast shock.
LightningThin short-lived channels and restrikes read more clearly than broad glowing tubes. The later full-path discharge remains visibly art-directed, and the generator is procedural rather than an electromagnetic simulation.
SandFine grains follow the shared emission, spread and fall. The corrected velocity support keeps the full active gesture within frame. The compact stream still has a ribbon-like outline in places.
MudRetains the stronger cohesive viscous carrier and wet surface. The inherited contact line remains visible after settling; this is a restrained material refinement rather than a new fluid solve.
PlantsConnected stems and textured leaves are a substantial structural change from the earlier particle vegetation. Unfurling and falling remain coherent through the clip. Reuse of the scanned shoots is still noticeable.
SnowA cohesive granular stream holds the gesture before falling and breaking up. It is visibly finer than large white beads, but the nearly uniform bright surface lacks convincing varied snow clumps.
GlassPersistent closed fragments form and release without the earlier topology swap. Internal reflections and sharp highlights establish glass, but the assembly remains dark on black and reads more as separate shards than a continuous glass body.
CrystalPreserves the stronger faceted fragment motion. Highlights and absorption distinguish the minerals, although individual pieces are small at the shared framing.
BloodThin sheets and fine breakup are retained. Saturated red highlights and the inherited fluid-domain boundaries near the end remain weaknesses; this is not a photoreal final.
HealingConnected plant tissue carries a localized repair front, using the corrected nested material. The repair is too subtle at gallery scale and still reads primarily as the plants study.
SpiritbendingConnected cyan channels narrow and relax instead of becoming smoke or water. The current silhouette still reads as a thin luminous cord and needs a stronger distinct identity.
EnergybendingInterwoven illuminated channels follow the shared gesture and decay. At gallery scale the strands merge into a pale cord, so this remains a graphic study and does not yet establish the requested energy-bending identity.
Lightning redirectionIncoming, guided, and outgoing phases are visibly distinct. Narrow branches and brief exposures avoid the earlier glowing-tube look. Channel construction remains procedural and intentionally follows the authored gesture.
Seismic senseDriven granular witnesses show the traveling elastic displacement and later fall. This removes the earlier leather-like surface, but is a stylized field diagnostic with no individual grain contact solve.
SoundDepth-integrated pressure gradients produce discrete expanding wavefronts rather than particle smoke. Their speed and optical sensitivity are authored for visibility; this is a scientific-style visualization, not naked-eye photoreal sound.
FlightThe wake remains within the shared framing and disperses. Short marker paths still look bristly near the source and remain below the desired realism. A volume reconstruction trial blurred into generic smoke and was excluded.
Spirit projectionThe original sigil remains coherent while a second copy travels away and dissipates. This preserves the brand shape, but is an authored luminous projection rather than a photoreal material.
Pressure / vacuumSparse rarefaction and rebound fronts replace the earlier dust cloud. These deliberately share the acoustic optical model; the result is a graphical diagnostic rather than a visible physical material.
HeatRefractive-gradient contours follow the solved hot plume and weaken as it cools and mixes. This replaces glowing particles with a numerical schlieren diagnostic; it is not a claim that warm air emits visible light.
