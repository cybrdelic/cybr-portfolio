# CYBRDELIC realism rebuild

Status: the user rejected the realism of the current material set. Rendering and playback checks are not evidence of realism.

The shared gesture remains an emitter/control path. A material must be allowed its own structure, inertia, deformation, breakup and optical response.

Core fire, water and air remain unchanged. Blue fire, steam and smoke were not included in the latest rejection and are preserved. Mud, crystal and blood need restrained refinement.

## Current evidence

Plants, steel, foam and ice have new still-frame construction tests in this folder. Plants and steel also have complete 1080p/30fps motion tests. Steel release fails stiffness/contact review; neither clip is an approved replacement. Foam still needs cell packing and drainage work. No new full batch has been presented as finished.

## Review gates

1. Recognizable material at the intended camera scale, before glow or atmosphere.
2. Plausible thickness, roughness, absorption, scattering and environment interaction.
3. No repeating placement pattern, constant tube radius, rigid decorative helix, or identical leaf/bubble library.
4. Temporal continuity and material-appropriate forces.
5. Sequence completeness and browser behavior, checked separately from visual realism.
